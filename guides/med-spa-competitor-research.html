COMPETITOR RESEARCH

How to Find Med Spa Competitor Gaps Without Guessing

Competitor research should reveal patterns and opportunities—not encourage blind copying.

Compare the offer

Record the treatments, introductory offers, packages and calls-to-action each competitor emphasizes.

Compare the customer journey

Open each website on a phone. Note how quickly you can understand the business, find treatment information and request an appointment.

Compare trust signals

Look at review volume and recency, credentials, real imagery, FAQs and other evidence presented to prospective customers.

Compare content

Look for recurring topics, treatment education, FAQs, seasonal promotions and content formats. Focus on what customers appear to need answered.

Turn observations into gaps

Prioritize gaps that are relevant to your business and realistically fixable. A competitor having more followers does not automatically mean their strategy is better.
