Med Spa Google Business Profile: What to Check First

Your Google presence is often the first step in a local treatment search. The goal is to make the profile accurate, useful and easy to act on.

Information accuracy

Check the business name, address/service area, phone, hours, website and category. Keep public information consistent.

Services

Review the services listed on the profile and make sure they reflect what the business actually offers.

Photos

Use current, authentic images that help a prospective client understand the environment and services. Avoid misleading imagery.

Reviews

Make it easy for genuine customers to leave feedback and respond professionally to reviews. Never buy or fabricate reviews.

Conversion path

Search visibility is only useful if visitors can take the next step. Check the website, call and appointment paths from a phone.

Measure outcomes

Track meaningful actions such as calls, appointment requests and form submissions where possible. Don't judge the profile only by impressions.
