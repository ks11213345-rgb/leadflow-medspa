Instagram + Booking Funnel Audit

Example: a fictional med spa with attractive social content but a weak bridge between content engagement and consultation enquiries.

Sample business: “Luna Aesthetics” — fictional. Purpose: demonstrate our methodology, not report real performance.

Executive summary

The fictional account posts consistently, but content is mostly awareness-focused. The profile does not make the offer, location and next step immediately clear, and individual posts rarely connect education to an appropriate enquiry action.

Profile positioning

Recommendation: make the bio answer three questions quickly: what the business offers, where it is located and what the visitor should do next.

Content-to-offer alignment

Organize content into useful buckets: treatment education, FAQs, proof, practitioner expertise, facility experience and appropriate offers. Each piece should have a clear purpose.

CTA system

Use one primary action per post. Examples include “Book a consultation,” “See treatment details,” or “Ask a question.” Avoid multiple competing CTAs.

Booking funnel test

Test the exact path from a Reel → profile → link → booking/enquiry. Record every click and question a new visitor must answer before submitting an enquiry.

Trust and compliance

Use authentic content and avoid misleading medical claims, guaranteed outcomes or deceptive transformations. Educational content should be reviewed for accuracy before publication.

Priority board

  • High: rewrite profile positioning
  • High: simplify the link/booking journey
  • High: connect content topics to specific services
  • Medium: create repeatable content pillars
  • Medium: track enquiry actions
