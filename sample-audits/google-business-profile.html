Google Business Profile Audit

Example: a fictional med spa with a solid reputation but an underdeveloped local discovery-to-enquiry path.

Sample business: “Luna Aesthetics” — fictional. Purpose: demonstrate our methodology, not report real performance.

Executive summary

The fictional profile contains the basics but leaves opportunities in service presentation, fresh imagery, review response quality and the path from discovery to the website or enquiry.

Information accuracy

Verify business name, address or service area, phone, hours, website and primary category. Keep important public details consistent across the business's online presence.

Services

Review whether important services are clearly represented. Prioritize the treatments customers actually search for rather than creating an oversized, confusing list.

Photos

Use recent, authentic images of the facility, team and relevant service environment. Keep imagery consistent with the actual customer experience.

Reviews

Build a system for requesting genuine reviews from real customers and responding professionally. Never purchase, fabricate or manipulate reviews.

Conversion path

Test the journey from Google discovery to website, call or booking on a phone. The profile should make the next step obvious and functional.

Priority board

  • High: verify core business information
  • High: improve primary service presentation
  • High: test mobile enquiry path
  • Medium: refresh authentic imagery
  • Medium: systematize genuine review requests
