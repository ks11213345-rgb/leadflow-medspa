Website Conversion Audit

Example: a fictional single-location med spa whose website receives local visitors but makes the next step harder than necessary.

Sample business: “Luna Aesthetics” — fictional. Purpose: demonstrate our methodology, not report real performance.

Executive summary

The fictional site has a credible service offering but its first screen is too broad, the primary action is not visually dominant, and visitors need several clicks to understand which treatment is right for them.

Priority 1 — Clarify the first screen

Observation: generic brand messaging appears before the main treatment categories. Recommendation: lead with location + core service category + a clear consultation CTA.

Priority 2 — Make the booking action persistent

Observation: the booking link is easy to miss on mobile. Recommendation: use a consistent primary CTA and verify that it opens a simple, mobile-friendly booking or enquiry path.

Priority 3 — Build treatment-specific decision pages

Observation: multiple services are grouped together with limited decision-support information. Recommendation: create focused pages that explain what each treatment is, who it may suit, what the process involves and how to ask questions.

Trust opportunities

  • Make credentials and practitioner information easy to find.
  • Use authentic facility and team photography.
  • Place relevant genuine reviews near decision points.
  • Avoid guaranteed outcomes or misleading before/after claims.

Measurement plan

Track meaningful actions: calls, consultation requests, booking starts and completed bookings. Compare conversion rates before and after changes rather than relying only on traffic.

Priority board

  • High: mobile CTA visibility
  • High: clearer homepage offer
  • High: treatment-specific pages
  • Medium: stronger trust placement
  • Medium: conversion-event tracking
